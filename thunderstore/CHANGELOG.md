# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Skills: `collect` picks up the items lying around; `plant` plants a field with the Cultivator, clearing
  stones and rocks in the way, and saves it to fill up later.
- Fights back when a monster comes at you while a skill runs; the Settings tab turns it off.
- The window (F7) and console commands (`autoverk run`, `autoverk stop`, `autoverk skills`).
