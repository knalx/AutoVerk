# Changelog

## 0.1.0

First release.

- Skills: `gather` picks the ripe plants around you, one kind or everything; `plant` plants a field with
  the Cultivator, clearing stones and rocks on the spots, and saves it to fill up later.
- Fights back when a monster comes at you while a skill runs, jumps when stuck on the way, and stops when
  your pack gets too heavy; the Settings tab turns each off.
- The window (F7) and console commands (`autoverk run`, `autoverk stop`, `autoverk skills`).
